What it showed: grouping pages by transitive closure (union-find) let a copy
cropped so badly that its dates were gone match several different
certifications at once and chain them into one group, so all but one were
dropped. The 15 copies left in were mostly cropped claim-form pages caught in
the same chains. Fix (commit after this one): anchor clustering, where a page
joins a group only by matching that group's most complete page. Because this
flaw was found by looking at the test split, that split is no longer held out;
the fixed method is scored on a freshly generated split, test2.
